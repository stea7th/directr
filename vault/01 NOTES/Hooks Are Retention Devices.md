# Hooks Are Retention Devices

A hook is not the first line of a video. It is a promise about the cost of scrolling away. The viewer isn't asking "is this interesting?" — they're asking "will I lose something if I leave?" A hook works when leaving feels like losing.

This reframes how hooks get written: instead of starting with what the video is about, start with what the viewer would miss. "How I edit my videos" is a topic. "Most people lose their best clips in the first edit" is a cost.

## Why This Matters
This is the core thesis behind Directr's positioning — "Fix your hook before you post." The product isn't a caption generator; it's a retention-risk detector that runs before publish, when fixing is still free. Every feature decision should trace back to this.

It's also the standard for my own scripts: if the first line doesn't name a cost, rewrite it before filming.
