# Hook Library

Every hook pattern worth reusing, with evidence. Processed hook captures from `00 CAPTURE` land here. See [[Hooks Are Retention Devices]] for the standard: a hook must name a cost of scrolling away.

## Format
For each pattern: the template, an example, and status (`untested` / `tested — result`).

## Patterns

### "Most people lose X the same way"
- Example: "Most people lose their best ideas the same way."
- Names a cost (loss) and implies the viewer is included.
- Status: untested

### "You're doing X in the wrong order"
- Example: "You're editing your video before fixing your hook."
- Cost: wasted effort. Directly on-brand for Directr.
- Status: untested
